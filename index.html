Personal Backup App

A private, single-user tool. Its owner uses it with rclone and restic to keep encrypted backups of their own data in their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy Policy

  • Data accessed: only files this app created in its owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see other Drive files.
  • Use: storing and retrieving the owner's encrypted backup files. Nothing else.
  • Storage: the Google authorization token is kept on the owner's private server. Backup contents are encrypted before upload.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.
  • Tracking: this page and the app use no analytics or cookies.
  • Revocation: access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
